## Findings by question

### 1. October weather
- Second inter-monsoon. Pattern: sunny or humid mornings, then **afternoon and evening thunderstorms**. The southwest coast and hill country are wettest. The east coast is the driest in early October and gets wetter late in the month as the NE monsoon builds.
- 2026 specifics: El Niño declared in June ([weather.lk](https://www.weather.lk/2026/07/16/noaa-predicts-high-probability-of-very-strong-el-nino-by-late-2026-what-it-means-for-sri-lanka/)). Severe drought in the southeast ([Sunday Times](https://www.sundaytimes.lk/260830/news/monaragala-hardest-hit-by-drought-as-yala-partially-closes-to-protect-wildlife-653661.html), [Taipei Times/Bloomberg](https://www.taipeitimes.com/News/biz/archives/2026/09/06/2003863747)). Met Dept outlook is above-normal rain from October ([NewsFirst](https://www.newsfirst.lk/2026/08/17/major-el-ni%C3%B1o-impact-on-sri-lanka-in-coming-months)). Thundershowers forecast from 1 Oct ([SL Chronicle](https://srilankachronicle.com/sri-lanka-braces-for-increased-thundershowers-from-october-1st-dmc-advises-caution/)).
- Per-town rainfall figures were only available from weather aggregators and are low-confidence, so they are not used on the site.

### 2. Arugam Bay
- Tail end of the Apr/May–Oct season. The likely breaks are Main Point, Whiskey Point, Pottuvil Point and Elephant Rock; Peanut Farm and Okanda need bigger swell ([Edgeway Surf](https://edgewaysurf.com/en/blog/arugam-bay-surf-season-october), [Lankanstays](https://lankanstays.com/blog/arugam-bay-surf-guide)).
- Many surf camps stay open to the end of October; some bars close, and crowds are very low.
- Security: 2024 advisories lifted ([Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/israel-removes-severe-travel-warning-for-sri-lanka-beach-town/), [Newswire](https://www.newswire.lk/2024/11/13/sri-lanka-reacts-to-us-revoking-security-alert/)). Extra police/STF presence since Mar 2026 ([Daily Mirror](https://www.dailymirror.lk/breaking-news/Low-number-of-Israeli-tourists-in-Arugam-Bay-amid-off-season-and-regional-tensions/108-334885)). Check um.fi before flying.
- Nearby: Lahugala Kitulana elephants and the Pottuvil lagoon mangrove boat ([Arugam Bay Agenda](https://www.arugambayagenda.com/wildlifetours/lagoon-safari-pottuvil/)). The 2026 operating status of both is not verified.

### 3. South coast
- Real season is Nov–Apr. From Jun–Oct onshore wind makes it messy; late October is a transition, with Weligama at about 2–4 ft ([Surf Atlas](https://thesurfatlas.com/sri-lanka-surf/sri-lanka-surf-season/), [Surf Without a Car: Weligama](https://surfwithoutacar.com/weligama-surf/)).
- Hiriketiya is sheltered and consistent most of the year ([Surf Without a Car](https://surfwithoutacar.com/hiriketiya-surf/), [Surf by Sachee](https://www.surfbysachee.com/post/surfing-hiriketiya-off-season-why-it-s-still-worth-the-trip)).
- Not verified: what exactly is open in Midigama/Kabalana in Oct 2026.

### 4. Yala, Kumana, Udawalawe
- Yala Blocks 1 & 2 closed 14 Sep–15 Oct 2026 ([Newswire](https://www.newswire.lk/2026/09/14/yala-national-park-blocks-1-and-2-closed-to-tourists-from-today/), [Lanka Newspapers](https://www.lankanewspapers.com/2026/08/26/yala-national-park-s-zones-1-and-2-set-to-close-for-tourists-from-september-14)). **Reopening not yet confirmed.** Recent years skipped the old routine Sep–Oct closure ([TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowTopic-g612381-i11956-k15367542-September_Closure-Yala_National_Park.html)).
- Kumana and Gal Oya campsites closed for drought ([Newswire](https://www.newswire.lk/2026/09/02/drought-forces-temporary-closure-of-campsites-at-kumana-and-gal-oya-national-parks/)).
- Udawalawe is open all year, with no 2026 closure found ([Udawalawa.com](https://www.udawalawa.com/blog/udawalawe-national-park-complete-guide/)).

### 5. Adam's Peak
- Off-season: unlit path, closed shops, leeches and poor visibility; go only with a guide and headtorches ([Lankanstays](https://lankanstays.com/blog/adams-peak-season-guide), [SL Spirit](https://www.srilanka-spirit.com/guides/adams-peak-when-to-go-pilgrim-season/)).
- Mahagiridamba landslide repaired ([SL Mirror](https://srilankamirror.com/news/mahagiri-damba-of-sri-pada-pilgrimage-route-renovated/), [Navy](https://news.navy.lk/eventnews/2026/01/05/202601051300/)).

### 6. Hill-country train
- Line status as of early Oct 2026: [Ada Derana (Nanu Oya–Badulla)](https://adaderana.lk/news/cmqm08w16000g356qwggwk9gp), [Daily Mirror (Jun)](https://www.dailymirror.lk/breaking-news/Upcountry-line-train-services-to-resume-from-Saturday/108-343187), [Daily Mirror (Gampola 2 Oct)](https://www.dailymirror.lk/breaking-news/Upcountry-train-services-to-Gampola-resume-from-Oct--2/108-351306), [Ada Derana (Dec target)](https://adaderana.lk/news/cmulb3vtz000l356o7gawwyx7), [Sunday Times](https://www.sundaytimes.lk/260621/news/podi-menike-hits-the-tracks-from-nanu-oya-to-badulla-marking-latest-phase-of-restoration-646913.html).
- Morning departure from Nanu Oya around 09:00, arriving in Ella around 11:30; a second train leaves around 14:30 ([byc.lk](https://byc.lk/nanu-oya-to-ella-train-2026)). Medium confidence; confirm at the station.
- Booking: [seatreservation.railway.gov.lk](https://seatreservation.railway.gov.lk/) opens 30 days ahead and needs passport numbers. Reserved seats sell out fast, and online booking for Nanu Oya–Ella was reported unavailable at times ([TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.in/ShowTopic-g293961-i8983-k15482912-Nanu_Oya_to_Ella_train_bookings_not_available-Sri_Lanka.html)). Unreserved 2nd/3rd class tickets bought at the station on the day are the backpacker fallback.
- The Ella Odyssey–Nanu Oya tourist train ran in 2025 ([Hiru News](https://www.hirunews.lk/en/396333/ella-odyssey-nanu-oya-train-service-begins-today)); its current status is not verified.

### 7. Kitulgala rafting
- Grade II–III, about 5 km on the Kelani, about 10:00–16:00 because of upstream dam releases. About USD 25–40 per person, or about USD 57–80 with canyoning ([SL Epic](https://srilankaepic.com/kitulgala-white-water-rafting-adventure-guide/), [Rapid Adventures](https://www.rapidadventures.lk/experiences/white-water-rafting-at-kitulgala), [SL Spirit](https://www.srilanka-spirit.com/guides/kitulgala-white-water-rafting-guide/)).
- Operators: Rafter's Retreat and Borderlands (both active per [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g608523-d607740-Reviews-Rafters_Retreat-Kitulgala_Sabaragamuwa_Province.html)). Belilena cave and canyoning nearby. Airport to Kitulgala takes about 1h45–2h20.

### 8. Ella
- Ella Rock about LKR 930 cash fee ([Daily Packers](https://thedailypackers.com/ella-rock/), [SL Spirit](https://www.srilanka-spirit.com/guides/ella-rock-sunrise-hike-detailed/)). Little Adam's Peak free ([Portuguese Traveler](https://theportuguesetraveler.com/sri-lanka/little-adams-peak-hike/)). Flying Ravana zipline ([rates](https://www.flyingravana.com/rates.html)). Lipton's Seat ([Laure Wanders](https://www.laurewanders.com/liptons-seat/)).
- Scene: Cafe Chill and 360 Bar on the main drag; party hostels ([Hostelgeeks](https://hostelgeeks.com/best-hostels-in-ella/), [Ella nightlife](https://adventurebackpack.com/ella-nightlife)). Nine Arch train times under the reduced timetable are low confidence ([Ella Guide](https://ellaguide.com/ella-train-times/)).
- Pekoe Trail: Stage 15 (Makulella → Ella) is open. About USD 10 per stage; check [thepekoetrail.org](https://thepekoetrail.org/pekoe-stages/).

### 9. Knuckles
- October is peak rain with heavy leeches; a registered guide and permit are required ([Knuckles Adventure](https://www.knucklesadventure.com/blog/best-time-to-visit-knuckles-mountain/), [Kate & Mike](https://www.kateandmikestravels.com/post/knuckles-mountain-range-sri-lanka-guide/)). Riverston reopened Feb 2026 ([TTW](https://www.travelandtourworld.com/news/article/riverston-in-sri-lanka-reopens-after-cyclone-ditwah-restoring-eco-tourism-access-to-the-knuckles-forest-reserve-with-new-safety-limits/)).

### 10. Practical
- **ETA:** free for Finns, 30 days, double entry; apply on [eta.gov.lk](https://eta.gov.lk) before departure ([VisasNews](https://visasnews.com/en/sri-lanka-makes-tourist-eta-free-for-40-nationalities-from-may-25/), [reminder](https://visasnews.com/en/sri-lanka-authorities-remind-travelers-to-obtain-an-eta-before-departure/)).
- **Drive times** (realistic, by van):

  | Leg | Time |
  |---|---|
  | Airport → Kitulgala | 1h45–2h20 |
  | Kitulgala → Kandy | 1h40–2h |
  | Kandy → Ella | 3.5–4.5h |
  | Ella → Arugam Bay | 3–4h |
  | Arugam Bay → airport | 6.5–8h |
  | Ella → Ahangama | 3–3.5h |
  | Ella → Hiriketiya | 2.5–3.5h |
  | Ahangama → airport | 2–3h |

  Sources: [rome2rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Arugam-Bay/Colombo-Airport-CMB), [Arugam Taxi](https://arugamtaxi.com/routes), [Visit Hiriketiya](https://www.visithiriketiya.com/getting-to/from-ella/), [Sola](https://solasrilanka.com/2026/08/13/how-to-get-from-colombo-to-ahangama/). Not sourced: **Kitulgala → Nanu Oya (via Hatton) is my estimate at about 3h.**
- **Van + driver:** about USD 70–120 per day; KDH about LKR 110–140/km. The rough week total for 6–8 guys is about **USD 600–900** (our estimate, not a quote). Driver room and board are usually free at hotels ([Oura Travel](https://ouratravel.com/cost-hire-driver-sri-lanka/), [Arugam Taxi](https://arugamtaxi.com/routes)).
- **Tuk-tuk self-drive:** IDP + AA Ceylon permit ([tuktukrental.com](https://tuktukrental.com/legal-drive-tuktuk-sri-lanka/), [Autoliitto IDP](https://www.kauppa.autoliitto.fi/Kansainvaelinen-ajokortti-1949)). About USD 15–25 per day.
- **Money and SIM:** 1 EUR ≈ 380 LKR (Sep 2026). BOC and People's Bank ATMs reportedly charge no fee. Dialog tourist SIM/eSIM at arrivals, LKR 1,399–2,450 for 20–50 GB ([guide](https://www.thesrilankanguideline.com/guides/sim-card)).
- **Vap Poya on Sun 25 Oct 2026:** dry day ([Lankanstays](https://lankanstays.com/blog/poya-days-sri-lanka)).
