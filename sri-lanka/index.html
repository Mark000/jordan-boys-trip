October reality check

Checked against news and operator sources on 3 Oct 2026. Some of our original assumptions were wrong, and the biggest one is the train.

The short version

October is inter-monsoon season, so it's the same story everywhere: warm, clear mornings, then thunderstorms in the afternoon or evening. Hike and surf early, and save the afternoons for beer and naps.

🚂 No Kandy → Ella train Changed

Cyclone Ditwah (Nov 2025) wrecked the hill line. Kandy/Gampola → Nanu Oya is still under repair, with a target of about 31 Dec. Nanu Oya → Ella is running again, and that's the best-looking stretch anyway. We drive up to tea country and take the train from there.

🌧️ El Niño ≠ dry October

2026 has a strong El Niño and a severe drought in the southeast. The Met Department still expects above-normal rain from October; the dry effect is expected mainly in Jan–Mar. Pack a rain jacket.

🏄 East vs south coast

Arugam Bay is at the tail end of its season: smaller swell, empty line-ups, some bars closing, and a 7–8h drive back to the airport. South coast is an improving shoulder season, Hiriketiya's sheltered bay works all year, and the airport is only 2–3h away.

🐆 Safari status

Yala Blocks 1 & 2 are closed 14 Sep–15 Oct because of the drought, and no reopening has been announced yet. Kumana campsites are closed. Udawalawe is open, sits right on the Ella → south coast road and is the elephant sure-thing.

⛰️ Adam's Peak is off-season

Pilgrim season starts in December. In October the path is unlit, the tea shops are shut, it's slippery and leechy, and the summit is likely cloudy. Not this time.

🍺 Sunday 25 Oct = dry day

It's Vap Poya, the full-moon holiday, so no alcohol is sold anywhere, hotel bars included. Plan a surf-and-coconut day, or stock up on Saturday.

The menu

Places

Ten candidates, each with an honest October verdict. Filter by verdict, then vote in the group chat.

Ella
1
Uva hills · 1,000 m

Ella

Backpacker HQ in the clouds: hostel rooftops, cheap kottu and a hike out of every door.

  • Ella Rock at sunrise, about 4h return; cash fee of about LKR 930 near the top. Little Adam's Peak (free) for sunset.
  • Nine Arch Bridge as a train goes over; timings changed after the cyclone, so ask at Ella station.
  • Flying Ravana zipline (about LKR 5,000), Ravana Falls, then Cafe Chill / 360 Bar.
  • Under the radar: Pekoe Trail Stage 15 (Makulella → Ella, about USD 10), and Lipton's Seat sunrise, 1–1.5h away by tuk-tuk.
GoHiking works in the mornings. The town is intact and running normally.
Nanu Oya → Ella
2
Hill country railway

Nanu Oya → Ella train

About 2.5h of tea estates, tunnels and waterfalls from an open train door. It's the best part of the famous ride.

  • The morning train leaves Nanu Oya around 09:00 and gets to Ella around 11:30; another leaves around 14:30. Confirm times locally.
  • Kandy → Nanu Oya is closed until about the end of 2026, so we cover that part by van (about 2.5–3h).
  • Reserved seats go on sale 30 days ahead on seatreservation.railway.gov.lk (passport numbers needed). If they're gone, buy unreserved 2nd/3rd class on the day.
  • Sleep the night before in Nuwara Eliya or a tea-estate bungalow: fireplace, arrack, cold mountain air.
GoThe full Kandy → Ella ride isn't possible this year, but this section is the highlight.
Kitulgala
3
Kelani river · ~2h from the airport

Kitulgala

White water through rainforest, the perfect jet-lag cure on day one or two.

  • Rafting on the Kelani: about 5 km of grade II–III, punchier in October's higher water. About USD 25–40 each.
  • Rafting + canyoning combo (natural slides and jumps): about USD 57–80.
  • Runs only about 10:00–16:00 because of the upstream dam, so book a morning slot.
  • Riverside lodges such as Rafter's Retreat; Belilena cave and jungle swims nearby.
GoOctober is good-water season, and it's right on the way into the hills.
Arugam Bay
4
East coast · Ampara

Arugam Bay

Barefoot surf town, end-of-season edition: empty line-ups, cheap rooms, sleepy bars.

  • Main Point and Whiskey Point for surfers, Elephant Rock for beginners and sunset. Peanut Farm needs a bigger swell.
  • Pottuvil lagoon mangrove boat at dawn: crocs, monitor lizards, birds.
  • Lahugala elephant drive at dusk. Kumana campsites are closed for the drought, so ask locally about day safaris.
  • Fresh fish and rice & curry on the beach, then a hammock.
GoTail end of the season, so swell is smaller and less reliable, and the drive back to the airport is 6.5–8h. Best if we fly mid-October.
Hiriketiya
5
South coast · Dikwella

Hiriketiya

Tiny horseshoe bay, hammocks under palms and the most sociable evenings on the coast.

  • A sheltered beach break that works most of the year: the best bet for surf lessons in late October.
  • Reef left at the edge of the bay for the better surfers.
  • Smoothie bowls, then sunset beers and a seafood barbecue.
  • About 2.5–3.5h from Ella, or about 2h from Udawalawe after a safari.
GoUpgraded from Maybe: it's the most reliable surf on the south coast in October, though a few bars run low-season hours.
Ahangama
6
South coast · Galle district

Ahangama & Galle

Surf village meets design cafés, with a 400-year-old fort down the road.

  • Kabalana's "The Rock" and Midigama reefs, if the onshore wind behaves.
  • Koggala stilt fishermen at sunrise; they expect a tip for photos.
  • Galle Fort: ramparts walk at sunset, then dinner and drinks inside the walls.
  • Only 2–3h to the airport on the expressway, which makes for an easy last day.
MaybeReal season starts in November. Late October is a shoulder period: improving, but wind can mess up the surf.
Udawalawe
7
Dry zone · between Ella and the south coast

Udawalawe safari

An open jeep among herds of wild elephants, no detour needed.

  • Afternoon jeep safari (about 3h); elephants are near-guaranteed.
  • Right on the road from Ella to Hiriketiya or Ahangama.
  • Takes Yala's place: Yala Blocks 1 & 2 were closed for the drought until 15 Oct, and no reopening was confirmed when we checked.
GoOpen all year, on our route, and a safe bet while Yala is uncertain.
Sigiriya
8
Cultural Triangle · ~2.5h north of Kandy

Sigiriya & Pidurangala

A sunrise scramble up Pidurangala, staring straight at Lion Rock.

  • Pidurangala at sunrise: a cheap ticket, a short boulder scramble at the top, and the best view of Sigiriya.
  • Sigiriya itself costs USD 35, so do one or the other.
  • Dambulla cave temples nearby. Hot-air balloons don't run until about 20 Oct.
MaybeFine weather-wise, but it's off both routes and costs about a day of driving. Only worth it if we drop the coast.
Knuckles
9
Central highlands · north-east of Kandy

Knuckles Range

Misty ridges, cloud forest and remote villages like Meemure.

  • Multi-day guided treks with a permit and a registered guide.
  • A day version exists: Corbett's Gap or Riverston (reopened Feb 2026).
  • October is peak rain here, and the leeches are legendary.
Skip this timeWet, leechy, and it eats days we don't have. Better on a Jan–Apr trip.
Adam's Peak
10
Sri Pada · near Hatton

Adam's Peak

The 2 am pilgrim climb, 5,500 steps to a sunrise above the clouds.

  • The season runs from the December full moon to May, when the path is lit and tea shops are open.
  • In October: no lights, shops shut, slippery steps and leeches, and a cloudy summit is likely.
  • The cyclone landslide on the Hatton path was repaired in Jan 2026.
Skip this timeDoable with a guide and headtorches, but low odds of a view for a 2 am start.

Boring but important

Practical

Entry: free ETA

  • Finns are on the free tourist ETA list (since May 2026): 30 days, double entry.
  • It's free but not automatic. Apply online before flying, about 1–2 weeks ahead.
  • Use eta.gov.lk only. Copycat sites charge USD 80–150 for it.

Getting around

  • Hire one van + driver for the week (KDH, 6–9 seats). About USD 70–120 a day, so very roughly USD 600–900 total for the group (our estimate; get 2–3 quotes).
  • Hotels usually house and feed the driver for free; check at small guesthouses.
  • PickMe and Uber work in Colombo and Kandy but are scarce in Ella. Tuk-tuks everywhere for short hops.

Drive times (real)

  • Airport → Kitulgala: 1h45–2h20
  • Kitulgala → Nanu Oya: ~3h (our estimate)
  • Ella → Arugam Bay: 3–4h
  • Ella → Hiriketiya: 2.5–3.5h · → Ahangama: 3–3.5h
  • Arugam Bay → airport: 6.5–8h
  • Ahangama → airport: 2–3h

Train tickets

  • Only Nanu Oya ↔ Ella is relevant; Kandy isn't connected until about the end of 2026.
  • Reserved seats: seatreservation.railway.gov.lk, 30 days ahead, passport numbers needed. They sell out fast, so book the moment our dates are fixed.
  • Plan B: unreserved tickets at the station on the day; you'll stand, but the doors are open.

Money & data

  • 1 EUR ≈ 380 LKR. Carry cash for tuk-tuks, shacks and hike fees.
  • BOC and People's Bank ATMs reportedly charge no fee.
  • Dialog SIM/eSIM at the arrivals hall: 20–50 GB for LKR 1,400–2,450.

Driving a tuk-tuk ourselves?

  • Needs a home licence + IDP (Autoliitto; the 1949 model is the safe pick) + an AA Ceylon permit from Colombo.
  • The 24/7 airport licence counter does not cover tuk-tuks.
  • About USD 15–25 a day. Fun for a day or two on the south coast; not for the whole route.

Pack list

Light rain jacket · reef boots · leech socks (Ella Rock, Pekoe, Kitulgala) · headtorch · quick-dry everything · a fleece for the tea-country night · sunscreen · dry bag for the phone · no hard-shell suitcases.
