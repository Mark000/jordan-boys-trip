## 1. October weather, El Niño 2026, drought

### Findings

Some farming districts had 85–100% rainfall deficits; govt says possibly worst in decades.

  - https://www.weather.lk/2026/07/16/noaa-predicts-high-probability-of-very-strong-el-nino-by-late-2026-what-it-means-for-sri-lanka/

- **Met Dept outlook for Oct–Dec 2026 is mixed / counter-intuitive:** Met Dept/NewsFirst (Aug 2026) reported that **from October rainfall is generally expected to be above normal across many parts of the island**, esp. if a positive IOD co-occurs, raising flood/landslide risk through Dec; El Niño's *dry* signal for Sri Lanka is mainly expected Jan–Mar 2027. Earlier (July) DG Ajith Wijemanne said dry weather may persist short-term but heavy rain possible Oct–Nov.
  - https://www.newsfirst.lk/2026/08/17/major-el-ni%C3%B1o-impact-on-sri-lanka-in-coming-months

- **Rain has started returning (late Sep / 1 Oct 2026):** Forecasts of afternoon thundershowers from 1 Oct in Uva, Eastern, Central provinces (fairly heavy >50 mm at places), a few morning showers on Western/Galle/Matara coasts; DMC advised caution about lightning.
  - https://srilankachronicle.com/sri-lanka-braces-for-increased-thundershowers-from-october-1st-dmc-advises-caution/

- **Climatological pattern (general knowledge, consistent with sources):** Second inter-monsoon (Oct–Nov) = island-wide convective afternoon/evening thunderstorms; mornings often clear. Typically the wettest period for the southwest and hill country; east coast is drier in early Oct but gets wetter late Oct→Nov as NE monsoon approaches.
  - Aggregator October averages (model/aggregator data, **low confidence**, some look off): Galle ~194 mm, Kandy ~301 mm, Nuwara Eliya ~656 mm (suspiciously high), Pottuvil ~177 mm, Batticaloa ~177 mm. https://www.weather-atlas.com/en/sri-lanka-weather-october , https://weather-and-climate.com/average-monthly-Rainfall-Temperature-Sunshine,pottuvil-lk,Sri-Lanka

  - Ordering (robust): Southwest coast & hill country wettest; east coast relatively drier in October but trending wetter late month.

## 2. Arugam Bay, late October

- **Season:** Surf season ~April–October; best Jun–Sep. October = late-season/transition, "fun surfable waves especially first half of month", swells less frequent, wind starts turning; from ~Nov swell disappears, wind goes onshore, much of village shuts.
  - https://edgewaysurf.com/en/blog/arugam-bay-surf-season-october
  - https://lankanstays.com/blog/arugam-bay-surf-guide , https://thesurfatlas.com/sri-lanka-surf/
  - Confidence: **med** (operator/blog sources, consistent with each other). vs assumption: **confirmed** that season is winding down; late Oct is marginal, not guaranteed.
- **Breaks:** Main Point (most consistent, crowded in peak, very uncrowded in Oct); Whiskey Point & Pottuvil Point (north, mellower, fewer people); Elephant Rock (gentlest); Peanut Farm & Okanda (south, need bigger swell → less likely to work in late Oct). Okanda is inside/next to Kumana NP area.
  - https://www.kateandmikestravels.com/post/arugam-bay-surf-spots-sri-lanka , https://edgewaysurf.com/en/blog/arugam-bay-surf-season-october
  - Confidence: **med**. Late-Oct swell consistency for 2026: **not verified** (no live Surfline/Surf-forecast data retrieved; check forecast ~7 days out).
- **What's open:** "Many surf camps and restaurants stay open until the end of October"; some seasonal bars/cafés start packing up by late Oct; established staples & surf schools year-round. Crowds low.
  - https://edgewaysurf.com/en/blog/arugam-bay-surf-season-october
  - Confidence: **med**. vs assumption: **confirmed** (quiet, partially closing).
- **Security:** Oct 2024 Israeli/US advisories (threat to Israeli tourists) → US lifted restrictions and Israel lowered level from 4 to 2 in Nov 2024.
  - https://www.timesofisrael.com/israel-removes-severe-travel-warning-for-sri-lanka-beach-town/ , https://www.newswire.lk/2024/11/13/sri-lanka-reacts-to-us-revoking-security-alert/ , https://www.ft.lk/Front-Page/Israel-removes-severe-travel-warning-for-Arugam-Bay/44-769257
  - 2026: Daily Mirror (10 Mar 2026) – heightened police/STF 24-hour security framework in Arugam Bay due to Middle East tensions since late Feb 2026; ~500 police/STF deployed; only 40–50 Israeli tourists. No new active Western advisory against Arugam Bay found.
  - https://www.dailymirror.lk/breaking-news/Low-number-of-Israeli-tourists-in-Arugam-Bay-amid-off-season-and-regional-tensions/108-334885 , https://www.arugam.info/2026/03/28/regional-tensions-are-drastically-impacting-israeli-tourism-in-sri-lankas-arugam-bay/

## 3. South coast (Ahangama, Hiriketiya, Weligama, Midigama, Kabalana) in October

- South coast surf season Nov–Apr; Jun–Oct is off-season with onshore SW monsoon wind making it messy. Late October = shoulder; spots "light up from late October". Weligama in Oct ~2–4 ft, mellow, beginner-friendly.
  - https://thesurfatlas.com/sri-lanka-surf/sri-lanka-surf-season/ , https://surfwithoutacar.com/weligama-surf/ , https://travel-surf.com/destination/sri-lanka/weligama/
  - Confidence: **med**. vs assumption ("onshore/messy in Oct"): **confirmed for early Oct, partly corrected for late Oct** (improving, shoulder; morning sessions best). Real season start: November.
- **Hiriketiya:** Sheltered horseshoe bay → one of the most consistent year-round spots; beach break for beginners + reef left-hander (shallow, holds ~6 ft) for intermediates. October described as shoulder (2–4 ft), some rain; trendy cafés/yoga/surf schools, but some bars/restaurants close in low season.
  - https://surfwithoutacar.com/hiriketiya-surf/ , https://thesurfatlas.com/sri-lanka-surf/hiriketiya-bay-surf/ , https://surfsouthsrilanka.com/dickwella-hiriketiya-bay-reef-break/ , https://www.surfbysachee.com/post/surfing-hiriketiya-off-season-why-it-s-still-worth-the-trip
  - Confidence: **med**.
- Midigama/Kabalana/Ahangama specifics for Oct 2026 (what's open, crowd): **not verified** beyond generic "shoulder season, quieter, some places reopening ahead of Nov". Ahangama has many year-round cafés/co-livings (general knowledge, not verified).
