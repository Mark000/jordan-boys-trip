## 10a. ETA/visa for Finnish citizens

- **Finland is on the free tourist ETA list** (40 countries, including FI, SE, NO, DK, UK, DE and US). Started **25 May 2026**. ETA is free, **valid 30 days, double entry**. Confidence: high. vs assumption: **confirmed**.
  - https://visasnews.com/en/sri-lanka-makes-tourist-eta-free-for-40-nationalities-from-may-25/
  - https://www.ivisa.com/news/2026-05-25-lk-sri-lanka-free-tourist-eta-40-nationalities
  - https://www.outlooktraveller.com/News/sri-lanka-offers-free-30-day-tourist-eta-to-visitors-from-40-countries-including-india
- **Still valid in Oct 2026?** The scheme is planned to run for **1 year (to about May 2027), with a review after 6 months (about Nov 2026)**. That means it should still apply in mid/late Oct 2026. Confidence: med-high. Re-check eta.gov.lk the week before flying.
  - https://traveltomorrow.com/sri-lanka-introduces-free-eta-scheme-for-tourists-from-40-countries/
- **This is not visa-free. You must apply for the ETA online BEFORE departure.** The authorities have said the ETA must be obtained before arrival, even though some portal pages still mention getting it on arrival. Apply 1–2 weeks ahead. Confidence: high.
  - https://visasnews.com/en/sri-lanka-authorities-remind-travelers-to-obtain-an-eta-before-departure/
  - https://visasnews.com/en/sri-lanka-parliament-approves-free-visas-for-40-countries-but-eta-remains-mandatory/
- **Official site is only https://eta.gov.lk** (Dept. of Immigration & Emigration). Copycat sites (eta.org.lk, srilankavisa.org.lk, srilanka-eta-visa.org etc.) charge USD 80–150 for an ETA that is free for Finns. Confidence: high.
  - https://www.srilanka-spirit.com/guides/sri-lanka-eta-visa-application-guide/
  - https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowTopic-g293961-i8983-k15001172-Fake_Sri_Lanka_Immigration_e_VISA_Portal-Sri_Lanka.html
- **VFS controversy (background):** In April 2024 the ETA was replaced by an outsourced e-Visa run by GBS/IVS/VFS. Fees rose and corruption was alleged. In Aug 2024 the Supreme Court suspended the deal, and eta.gov.lk was reactivated in late Sept 2024. It is the portal in use now. Confidence: high.
  - https://www.sundaytimes.lk/240804/news/online-visa-process-suspended-by-sc-566208.html
  - https://visasnews.com/en/sri-lanka-the-eta-portal-is-reactivated/
  - https://www.business-standard.com/finance/personal-finance/sri-lanka-suspends-its-new-e-visa-system-3-months-after-launch-here-s-why-124080600885_1.html
- Non-scheme nationalities pay **USD 50** for a tourist ETA (relevant only if someone in the group holds another passport). Confidence: med. https://www.eta.gov.lk/slvisa/visainfo/fees.jsp?locale=en_US

## 10c. Private driver / van and ride-hailing

- **Day rates (2025–26):** car with SLTDA driver USD 50–70/day. Van USD 70–120/day. Minibus for up to 9 pax quoted from about USD 180/day by premium operators. Quoted rates usually include fuel, tolls, parking and the driver's food and lodging. Confidence: med.

- **Per-km (local listings, KDH):** about LKR 110–140/km for 1–2 day trips. Example: LKR 13,000/day including 250 km, then LKR 70/km extra. Night parking about LKR 1,000. Confidence: med (local classifieds).

## 10d. Tuk-tuk self-rental

- **Legal requirements:** home licence + **IDP** + a Sri Lankan permit. The permit is either (a) the **AA Ceylon Recognition Permit**, which needs an IDP since about March 2025 and covers tuk-tuks if the IDP has category A or B, or (b) a DMT temporary licence. An IDP alone is not enough. Confidence: med-high.
  - https://tuktukrental.com/legal-drive-tuktuk-sri-lanka/ , https://tuktukrental.com/how-to-get-an-international-licence-in-sri-lanka/ , https://www.rapidadventures.lk/blog/sri-lanka-driving-permit-for-foreigners
- **Airport DMT counter** (24/7 at CMB arrivals since 3 Aug 2025): LKR 15,000 for 1 month. It **does NOT cover three-wheelers (tuk-tuks)**. For tuk-tuks use AA Ceylon (Colombo) or DMT Werahera. Confidence: med.
  - https://nohatuktuk.com/new-rule-sri-lankan-driving-licenses-for-foreigners/ , https://www.localrent.com/en/journal/sri_lanka/articles/local-temporary-license/
- **AA permit cost:** about LKR 7,700–11,000 (about USD 25–40), processed in 15–60 min. Rental firms often arrange it. Confidence: med (sources vary). https://advice.lk/tourist-driving-permit/
- **What Finns need:** Autoliitto issues both IDP models: **1949 Geneva (€37, 1-year validity)** and **1968 Vienna (€47, 3 years)**. Finland is party to both conventions. I found **no source stating which model Sri Lanka requires.** Sri Lanka is historically a 1949 Geneva party (not verified in this session), so the **1949 IDP is the safe choice**. Make sure category A/B is covered. Order it from Autoliitto before leaving; it is not available in Sri Lanka. Sri Lanka-specific model requirement: **not verified**.
  - https://www.kauppa.autoliitto.fi/Kansainvaelinen-ajokortti-1949 , https://www.autoliitto.fi/mita-eroa-on-vuoden-49-ja-vuoden-68-kortilla , https://traficom.fi/en/drivers-and-vehicles/driving-licenses/international-driving-licence
- **Rental cost:** about USD 15–25/day (short rentals about USD 22/day). Usually includes insurance, unlimited km, a 1-hour driving lesson and hotel delivery. Delivery/pickup in Ella about USD 40–60. Confidence: med.
  - https://atickettotakeoff.com/en/renting-a-tuk-tuk-in-sri-lanka/ , https://www.roamingintheknow.com/sri-lanka/renting-tuktuk-sri-lanka , https://tuktukslr.com/price/
- **Verdict for a 7-day trip:** the paperwork (IDP + AA visit in Colombo) and 3 people per tuk-tuk make self-drive tuk-tuks across the whole trip impractical. A 1–2 day local tuk-tuk "rally" on the south coast (Ahangama to Hiriketiya) is a fun option if one or two guys get the IDP + AA permit.

### CRITICAL: Kandy–Ella train is NOT running end-to-end (Oct 2026)
- Cyclone Ditwah (Nov 2025) damaged the upcountry line in 200+ places. As of early Oct 2026:
  - Colombo–Peradeniya–Gampola services resumed only on **2 Oct 2026**.
  - **Nanu Oya–Ella–Badulla reopened 18 July 2026.**
  - **Kandy/Gampola–Nanu Oya is still under repair**, with full reopening expected end-2026 or early 2027.
- **The classic Kandy to Ella train is therefore not possible**, and the Ella Odyssey Kandy–Ella service is effectively not running on its full route (not verified for Odyssey specifically). **Workaround:** drive Kandy to Nanu Oya (about 2h), then take the train Nanu Oya to Ella (the most scenic part, via Haputale). Alternatively, ride Ella to Demodara or Haputale as a short scenic hop. Confidence: med-high. vs assumption: **corrected**.

## 11. Experiences, rated for October and for this group

**October weather context:** October is the inter-monsoon month. Expect afternoon thunderstorms island-wide. The south/west coast is improving. The east coast (Arugam) is at the tail end of its season, with the NE monsoon arriving Oct–Nov. https://www.srilanka-maldives.com/sri-lanka-weather-in-october/ , https://lankanstays.com/blog/sri-lanka-in-october

### Strong picks (top 7)
1. **Kitulgala white-water rafting + canyoning.** Oct suitability: **5/5** (May–Dec is the high-water "thrill" season). Fit: **5/5**. About USD 25–40 for rafting; USD 57–80 for a rafting + canyoning combo. Grade 2–3 rapids, 5 km stretch. Confidence: med-high. https://srilankaepic.com/kitulgala-white-water-rafting-adventure-guide/ , https://www.kitulgalaadventures.com/
2. **Pekoe Trail day stages (Ella/Haputale area).** Oct: **3.5/5** (rain and leeches; go mornings). Fit: **5/5**. A 300+ km, 22-stage trail; Stage 15 (Makulella to Ella) is fully open. Stage 13 (Haputale to St Catherine's) has some overgrowth. Stages 11 and 12 were on "Amber" alert. Trail pass is about **USD 10 per stage**, bought on the official site. Check live status at thepekoetrail.org. Confidence: med. https://thepekoetrail.org/pekoe-stages/ , https://thepekoetrail.org/category/trail-update/ , https://pulseofsrilanka.com/blog/pekoe-trail-sri-lanka-guide/
3. **Diyaluma Falls upper infinity pools** (between Ella and Haputale/Koslanda). Oct: **2.5/5**. Oct–Nov is the heaviest-flow period, so swimming may be unsafe; take a local guide. There have been fatal falls off the 220 m edge. Fit: **5/5** on a dry day. Confidence: med. https://www.thesrilankanguideline.com/posts/diyaluma-falls-complete-guide-sri-lanka , https://www.thecommonwanderer.com/blog/guide-to-diyaluma-falls-sri-lanka
4. **Nanu Oya to Ella scenic train** (instead of Kandy–Ella). Oct: **4/5**. Fit: **4/5**. This is the best scenery section and it is open (see the critical note above). Confidence: med-high.
5. **South-coast surf lessons + chill (Hiriketiya / Ahangama).** Oct: **4/5**. Mellow 2–4 ft waves, good for beginners. Hiriketiya's sheltered bay works year-round. Fit: **5/5** (beer, surf, cafés). Confidence: med. https://beachynest.com/hiriketiya-beach-surfing-guide/ , https://surfwithoutacar.com/hiriketiya-surf/
6. **Arugam Bay late season + Lahugala elephants / Kudimbigala / Okanda.** Oct: **3/5**. The surf season (Apr–Oct) is winding down: uncrowded lineups, but fewer swells, afternoon storms and some venues reducing operations. Lahugala elephant tour costs about LKR 40,500 per car. Kumana campsites are closed (drought). Fit: **4/5**. Confidence: med. https://edgewaysurf.com/en/blog/arugam-bay-surf-season-october , https://trustker.com/sri-lanka-day-tours/lahugala-elephant-area-tour-from-arugam-bay/ , https://www.roughguides.com/sri-lanka/east/arugam-bay-around/
7. **Colombo rooftop bars + arrack cocktails** (arrival or last night). Oct: **5/5** (indoor/rooftop, but avoid Sun 25 Oct, Poya). Options: Cloud Red (26th floor), ON14, Vistas, Botanik, ColomBar for arrack cocktails. Fit: **4/5**. Confidence: med. https://www.bluelankatours.com/blog/who-loves-a-tropical-rooftop-bar-these-are-the-best-in-colombo , https://www.timeout.com/sri-lanka/bars-pubs

### Skip / poor fit for October 2026
- **Mirissa whale watching.** Season is **Nov–Apr**, so late Oct is borderline. https://pickyourtrail.com/blog/whale-watching-mirissa
- **Kalpitiya kitesurfing.** October is the unreliable tail of the May–Oct season (about 14 kn average, light wind until mid-Oct). It is also off-route. https://kitetrip-planner.com/en/kitesurfing-kalpitiya/
- **Dambulla hot-air ballooning.** **Not operating 1 Jun–20 Oct**; the season runs Nov–May. https://lakpura.com/products/hot-air-ballooning-from-dambulla
- **Pigeon Island / Trincomalee.** The season is roughly May–Sep. October is the transition to the NE monsoon, and it is off-route. Not verified this session.
- **Kandy Esala Perahera.** Held in Jul/Aug, so not in October.
- **Yala.** Blocks 1–2 are closed until 15 Oct 2026 (drought).
- **Cricket.** I could not verify any home international in Sri Lanka in mid/late Oct 2026. The national team plays T20Is in Pakistan around 9–11 Oct. Not verified. https://www.cricinfo.com/team/sri-lanka-8/match-schedule-fixtures-and-results
- **Sigiriya / Pidurangala.** Fine in October (Pidurangala at sunrise is the cheap alternative to the USD 35 Sigiriya ticket), but it is about 2.5h north of Kandy and off both routes. Only worth it if you trade it for Arugam.
- **Bambarakanda, Sinharaja, cooking classes, stilt fishing, Galle Fort:** not researched in depth this session (not verified). Galle Fort and stilt fishing sit right next to Ahangama and are easy add-ons.
